## Design System

**Colors**
- Background: warm white (#FAF8F5)
- Primary text / headlines / primary buttons: black (#1A1A1A)
- Secondary text / labels / dividers / secondary accents: vintage brown (#8B6F47)
- No other colors. No red, no navy, no gradients, no shadows.

**Typography**
- One font family throughout, sans-serif only (e.g., Space Grotesk or General Sans)
- Headlines: bold weight, black
- Body/labels: regular/light weight, vintage brown or black depending on hierarchy
- No serif fonts anywhere

**Layout principles**
- Single column, mobile-first, generous whitespace/padding
- Thin 1px black or brown hairlines instead of boxes, cards, or drop shadows
- Buttons: solid black or vintage brown fill, white text, flat (no shadow, no gradient)

## Core Principle: No Wordiness, No Instructions

Rewrite the entire UI to remove instructional/explanatory text. The app should be usable purely through visual cues, icons, and obvious placement — a first-time user should never need a sentence of guidance.

Apply this to every screen:
- Remove all instructional sentences (e.g., "Take one to four photos. Imperfect is the whole point.")
- Remove the "FRAME 01/03" label
- Replace the numbered step indicator with a plain thin progress bar (3 segments, vintage brown fill, no numbers or text)
- Camera/capture screen: no instructional text at all — just the camera view and a shutter button (icon only)
- Style screen: filter/style options shown as visual thumbnails the user taps — no explanatory copy, just the previews
- Note + save screen: a single minimal text input with a placeholder like "add a note" (placeholder text only, not a full instruction), plus Save/Share buttons (icon + short label only, e.g., a download icon next to "Save")
- Landing page: app name + one-line tagline max, then straight into the "make one" action — no feature list, no explanation paragraph
